### CAREGIVERS-TRAINING-GRANT: Caregivers Training Grant (CTG)

https://aic.sg/financial-assistance/caregivers-training-grant (**description changed**)

Old:

> The Caregivers Training Grant is a $200 annual subsidy that lets caregivers attend approved courses to better care for their loved ones. The $200 subsidy is provided for each care recipient every financial year (from April to March the following year). If multiple caregivers of the same care recipient wish to attend training, the $200 would need to be shared among the family members.

New:

> The Caregivers Training Grant (CTG) helps caregivers attend approved courses to better care for their loved ones. Each care recipient gets a $200 top-up every financial year (April to March), and the balance is capped at $400. First-time users start with $400. If several caregivers of the same care recipient attend training, they share the grant.

### HOME-CAREGIVING-GRANT: Home Caregiving Grant (HCG)

https://www.aic.sg/financial-assistance/home-caregiving-grant (**description changed**)

Old:

> The Home Caregiving Grant (HCG) has been enhanced on 1 March 2023, increasing the HCG payouts from the existing $200 per month to up to $400 per month. This is to recognise caregivers’ contributions and further reduce caregiving costs. with more help provided to the lower-income.

New:

> The Home Caregiving Grant (HCG) gives caregivers of persons with moderate to severe disabilities a monthly cash payout of $200 to $600 to help with caregiving costs. Lower-income households receive more: $600 a month for a per-capita household income of up to $1,500.

- `phone` has mixed types ({'NoneType': 74, 'str': 47, 'list': 6}): sometimes a string, sometimes a list.
